How to read this

India: the score bands below combine reported class averages / competitive benchmarks. For example, IIMA's current PGP process lists a 665 GMAT Focus minimum for that specific eligibility route, while many other Indian programmes do not publish a simple hard cutoff.

Global: a 650–699 can be viable at many strong programmes, but the M7/top US schools typically sit much higher. A 700+ score gives substantially more flexibility, but profile strength still matters.
